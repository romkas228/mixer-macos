## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: a finished macOS application icon for Mixer, a lightweight native per-application audio mixer. Square 1024 × 1024 design, production-ready standalone artwork.
Primary request: an original, exceptionally polished modern Apple-platform-style app icon, with the clarity and restraint of a built-in macOS utility. Do not copy an existing icon or use an Apple logo.
Subject: one centered softly rounded superellipse app tile, with exactly three bold vertical audio mixer faders. Each fader has a simple rounded recessed track and a wide, softly rounded silver-white adjustment knob. Put the three knobs at clearly different heights. Keep the symbol extremely legible at small sizes.
Style and materials: subtle dimensional glass and cool anodized aluminum, crisp edges, delicate highlights and precise soft shadows. Rich sapphire-blue to luminous azure tile, ivory/silver fader symbol, a restrained hint of cyan in the reflections. Premium and calm, not a toy, not exaggerated neon.
Composition: perfectly straight-on front view, symmetrical placement of the three tracks, no perspective tilt. The tile occupies about 84% of the image width and height, centered with generous balanced transparent margins. A faint short shadow may sit immediately beneath the tile.
Background: truly transparent outside the rounded tile; preserve alpha, no backdrop or ground plane.
Constraints: no text, letters, numbers, words, logos, watermark, waveform, music note, extra UI elements, ornamental details, mockup, computer screen or icon grid. Deliver one single finished icon.
